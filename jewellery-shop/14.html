Our Craft

Every link is set by hand, the old way

A kamarbandh is not stitched or stamped — it is built link by link, stone by stone, over weeks of quiet, patient work. We partner directly with karigar families to keep that tradition alive.

  • BIS hallmark certified gold plating on brass & silver base
  • Adjustable hook-and-chain sizing, fits 26"–40" waist
  • Comes with a velvet keepsake box & authenticity card
  • Free resizing & lifetime exchange against gold weight
