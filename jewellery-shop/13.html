Know Your Ornament

The Anatomy of a Hath Phool

01

Kada — The Wrist Bracelet

The anchor of the piece, worn snug at the wrist. Ours are hinged for an easy clasp under bridal sleeves.

02

Phool — The Palm Medallion

The "flower" that rests on the back of the hand, usually the most heavily worked piece in kundan or polki.

03

Zanjeer — The Finger Chains

Delicate strands that run from the medallion to each finger ring, said to represent protection extended to the bride.

04

Aarsi — The Mirror Ring

A small mirrored ring on the index finger, once used by brides to catch a first glimpse of the groom.
