Our Craft

Born of temple bells,
carried into everyday life.

The jhumka traces its shape to the bell motifs carved into Chola-era temples — a jingle believed to ward off evil with every step of the dance. At Jhumka Classic, our artisans in Jaipur and Hyderabad still hand-set every kundan stone and hand-hammer every dome, the way it's been done for generations.

No two pieces are identical. That's not a flaw in production — it's the proof of a human hand behind it.

"Every jhumka should sound like the person wearing it — a little bold, a little musical."
