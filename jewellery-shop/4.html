Our Craft

Set by hand, one kundan at a time.

Each maang tikka begins as a sheet of gold-plated brass or silver, shaped by karigars who've inherited the craft across three generations. Stones are set in the traditional kundan technique — no glue, only pressure and precision.

From our ateliers in Jaipur and Hyderabad to your maang: every piece is inspected twice before it leaves for your wedding trunk.
