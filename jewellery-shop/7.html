Craftsmanship

Set by hand, in Jaipur

Each Solitaire passes through four hands before it leaves the bench: one to cut the claws, one to seat the stone, one to polish the shank, and one to check it under magnification before it's ever photographed.

The four-claw setting is deliberately minimal — enough metal to hold the diamond secure through decades of wear, and not a milligram more.

  • Platinum 950 or 18K gold, cast and finished in-house
  • Conflict-free stones, traceable to origin
  • Hand-engraved with a private serial inside the band
Sizing & Care

Getting the fit right

How do I find my ring size?+

Wrap a strip of paper around the base of your finger, mark where it overlaps, and match the length in millimetres to our size chart — or visit any local jeweller for a free measurement.

Can I resize it later?+

Yes. Every Solitaire includes one complimentary resizing within 60 days of delivery, up or down by two half-sizes.

How should I care for the setting?+

Remove it before swimming or heavy lifting, clean it monthly with warm water and a soft brush, and have the claws checked once a year.
