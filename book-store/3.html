How Acquisition Works

Every volume is sourced, not stocked.

We do not warehouse. Each title in a release has been located, condition-graded, and — where needed — restored by our in-house binder before it ever reaches the shelf.

  • 01 A wanted list is opened for the season's release.
  • 02 Volumes are sourced from estate and private sales.
  • 03 Binding, endpapers, and slipcase are restored in-house.
  • 04 The edition is numbered and released by appointment.
