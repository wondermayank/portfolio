Aftercare

Three habits, three extra weeks of bloom.

Cut at an angle

Trim 2cm off each stem at a 45° angle every second day, using clean shears.

Change the water

Fresh, cool water every two days keeps the stems from clouding and softening.

Keep it out of the sun

Direct light and nearby fruit both shorten a lily's life — a cool corner suits it best.
