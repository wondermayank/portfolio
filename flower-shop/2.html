Keeping your sunflowers bright.

Trim the thick stems

Cut 2–3cm off each hollow stem at an angle, and re-trim every 2 days for the best water uptake.

Give them real light

Unlike most cut flowers, sunflowers like bright rooms — just keep them off a hot windowsill.

Change the water often

Sunflowers drink a lot — top up and refresh the water every 1–2 days with the enclosed flower food.
