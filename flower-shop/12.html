Since 2019

Every stem is knotted, not stuffed into foam.

Bloom & Twine started on a balcony with three hooks and a bundle of dried statice. Today our florists still hand-tie every hanger the same way — natural fibre cord, a breathable frame, and blooms chosen to dry gracefully instead of wilting.

Because nothing is glued or foam-set, most pieces last 8–14 months and only get more textured with age.
