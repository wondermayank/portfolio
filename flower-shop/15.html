Our Story

Grown slow. Dried by hand. Styled to last.

Everbloom started on a windowsill full of flowers we couldn't bear to throw away. We began hang-drying leftover stems, and within a season, friends were asking us to make arrangements for their own homes.

Today every bouquet, wreath and vase arrangement is still dried and styled entirely by hand in small batches — never mass-produced, never artificial.

  • 01 Ethically sourced from local growers and our own cutting garden.
  • 02 Air-dried slowly over 2–3 weeks to hold natural color and shape.
  • 03 Hand-finished and packed in reusable, compostable wrap.
