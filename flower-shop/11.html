Petal & Vow began as a single flower cart and grew into a full bridal atelier. We work with seasonal roses, peonies, garden hydrangea and dried botanicals sourced fresh each week, hand-tied the morning of your wedding so every bloom is at its best.

From the first sketch to the final ribbon, every bouquet is built around your gown silhouette, your venue and the colours that mean something to you.

How It Works

From sketch to "I do"

STEP ONE

Consultation

We talk gown, palette, venue and season — then sketch two or three bouquet directions built for you.

STEP TWO

Design & Trial

A trial bouquet is hand-tied so you can see and hold the exact blooms before the wedding week.

STEP THREE

Wedding-Day Delivery

Cut, conditioned and tied the morning of, delivered fresh to you and your bridal party on time.
