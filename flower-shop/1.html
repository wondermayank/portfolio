Keeping your roses at their best.

Trim at an angle

Cut 2cm off each stem at a 45° angle before placing in water, and re-trim every 2 days.

Cool, out of the sun

Keep away from direct sunlight, heaters and fruit bowls — ripening fruit ages flowers faster.

Fresh water, every 2 days

Use the enclosed flower food and change the water regularly to add days to their life.
