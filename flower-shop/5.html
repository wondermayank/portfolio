Our Story

Strung by hand, styled for the festival

Marigold Decor Festive began as a single flower stall at a temple market, stringing garlands before sunrise for families preparing for weddings and Diwali. Today we grow that same devotion into every order — real marigold, real leaves, no plastic shortcuts.

Every piece is made to order on the morning it ships, so what arrives at your door still smells like the field.

  • Farm-fresh marigold, cut daily
  • Hand-strung by local artisans
  • Custom mandap & venue styling
  • Eco-friendly, plastic-free packing
