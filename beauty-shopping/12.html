How to layer it

The order matters more than the products

01

Cleanse & prep

Start on damp skin. A clean, balanced base is what lets every layer after this actually absorb instead of sitting on top.

02

Hydrate & veil

Pat the essence in, then press — never rub — the moisturizer through. This is where the “glass” texture actually builds.

03

Seal & glow

Finish with a few drops while skin is still tacky. Lock the moisture in, let the light sit on the surface.
