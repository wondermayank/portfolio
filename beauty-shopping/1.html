The Art of
Airbrush HD

Airbrush HD makeup is a revolutionary technique that uses a precision compressor to spray ultra-fine pigment particles onto the skin — creating a finish so natural it looks like flawless bare skin, yet covers everything perfectly.

At Makeup Studio by Suu, our certified artists in Frazer Town, Bangalore, blend international techniques with Indian bridal aesthetics to create looks that honour your heritage while keeping you radiant all day and night.
