Our Craft

A dot with more meaning than its size suggests

The bindi has marked the space between the brows for centuries — a mark of auspiciousness, of attention, of the third eye. We work with three family workshops in Jaipur and Surat who still cut velvet and set stone by hand.

Every batch is tested for skin-safe adhesive and humidity resistance, so it survives a Mumbai monsoon wedding as easily as a Delhi winter pooja.
