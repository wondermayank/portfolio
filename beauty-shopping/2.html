Drama that speaks before you do.

Application Guide

How to Achieve the Look

Follow our step-by-step guide, curated by Luxora's in-house beauty editors and professional makeup artists.

01

Prime & Prep

Apply an eyeshadow primer across the lid to the brow bone. This ensures pigment stays vibrant and crease-free through the evening.

02

Lay the Base Shadow

Pack a mid-tone taupe across the lid with a flat shader brush. Build coverage gradually to avoid over-application.

03

Build the Smoke

Using a fluffy blending brush, work deep charcoal or black into the outer corner and crease in circular buffing motions for a seamless gradient.

04

Line & Define

Apply kohl liner along the upper lash line — smudge for that lived-in, sultry edge. Tight-line the waterline with a white pencil to open the eye.

05

Highlight & Finish

Press a champagne shimmer to the inner corner and brow bone. Finish with volumising mascara, building three coats from root to tip.

Pro Tip
"Apply shadow before foundation — fallout wipes away cleanly."
