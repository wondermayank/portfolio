The Technique

A face read like a topographic map

Every shoot starts with the same three readings — where light naturally lands, where it naturally falls away, and where the two should meet.

Fig. 01 — Contour MapElevation: Light → Shadow
01

Elevate

A pale, cool highlight along the brow bone, bridge, and cupid's bow lifts the plane of the face toward camera.

02

Recede

A warm contour traces the hollow of the cheek and the temple, pushing that plane back into shadow.

03

Resolve

A damp sponge dissolves the line between the two — the step most shoots skip, and the one that reads as bone, not paint.
