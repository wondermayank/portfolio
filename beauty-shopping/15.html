The Ritual

Three minutes, twice a week

Brow care isn't a routine to perform. It's three short steps that quietly compound.

First

Shape the line

Glide the razor along the natural arch, against the grain, in short strokes. The comb guard keeps you from taking too much.

Then

Tweeze the stragglers

Catch what the razor couldn't reach. Pull in the direction of growth, close to the root, one hair at a time.

Finally

Feed the follicle

Pat the density serum along the brow bone before bed. Let it work while you sleep, not under makeup.
