How to wear it

Build your
bare look

1

Prep with moisture

Start with clean, hydrated skin. Our Skin Tint adheres best over moisturizer — giving that lit-from-within finish.

2

Press, don't rub

Dot the Skin Tint across cheeks, nose, and forehead — blend with fingertips using gentle pressing motions.

3

Color in one swipe

Tap Lip & Cheek Flush onto the apples of cheeks and lips. Use a finger to blend for a natural, monochromatic flush.

4

Set and go

Mist your face from 12 inches away. Let dry — and that's your whole morning routine done.
