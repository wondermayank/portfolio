The Line of the Look
Editor's Note

A crease is just a decision, drawn in colour.

Every cut crease begins the same way: a transition colour swept through the socket, then erased along one clean edge. What's left behind isn't a gradient — it's a border. Lid colour on one side, bare skin or a contrasting shade on the other, with nothing soft between them.

For this issue, we asked four artists to treat that border as the entire concept. Not a finishing touch on a smoky eye, but the headline. The sharper the cut, the louder the statement — and the more it depends on having the right tools in hand before the blending sponge ever comes out.

"It's the one technique where a single shaky millimetre changes the whole story of the eye." — Lead Artist, Issue 01
