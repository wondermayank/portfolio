The Method

From two products to one fade, in three moves.

No lip liner precision needed — the system is built to forgive a shaky hand.

Anchor the outline

Trace the outer edge and corners of your lips with the Berry Core Stick. Keep it light — this is your depth, not your full coverage.

Light the center

Pat the warmer center shade directly onto the middle of your lower lip, where light naturally catches.

Melt the line

Run the Soft-Melt Brush outward from center to corner in small circles until the seam disappears.
