Our Heritage
Where every thread tells a thousand years of story.

Chanderi, the ancient town of Madhya Pradesh, has been the cradle of India's most exquisite weaves since the 13th century. Wondermayank brings together Chanderi's legendary lightness with Chanderi's opulent silk and zari — a union of two great traditions, worn by queens and reimagined for today.
