The Ikat is Not
Printed — It is
Remembered

In tribal tradition, Ikat is not a textile. It is a vocabulary. The weaver ties and dyes the threads before a single pass of the shuttle — building the image from memory, from ancestral knowledge, from the land itself.

Ikat Saree Tribal curates only those weaves where this unbroken chain holds — where the maker knows the meaning of every motif they repeat.
