A Complete Visual Guide

How to Wear
a Saree

Ten visual steps to drape a cotton saree with elegance — the classic Nivi style, beloved across India.

1 Step One Get Ready
Step 1
1
Step 01
Get Ready — Dress First
Wear a well-fitted blouse and a petticoat tied firmly at the waist. Choose a petticoat colour that closely matches your saree. Stand straight and ensure the petticoat hem falls just above ankle length for the best drape.
💡 Tip: A contrasting blouse can make the saree look more vibrant and festive.
Step 2
2
Step 02
First Tuck — Start Right
Tuck the plain end of the saree into the petticoat at the centre-front (navel area), and wrap the saree once around your body in an anticlockwise direction. The saree should stay about 12 inches above the floor.
Step 3
3
Step 03
Wrap Around — One Full Round
Continue to wrap the saree fully around your body from right to left. Keep the lower border even throughout. Tuck the entire wrapped portion firmly and evenly into the petticoat waistband to secure the base layer.
Step 4
4
Step 04
Make Pleats — The Art
Take the remaining fabric and make 5–7 neat pleats of about 5 inches each. Fold each pleat in the same direction (towards the left) and keep them even and uniform. The pleats are the most artistic part of a saree drape.
💡 Tip: Use your left hand to hold all pleats together. Keep folds facing left for the classic Nivi style.
Step 5
5
Step 05
Tuck Pleats — Secure Neatly
Hold all the pleats together and tuck them neatly into the waistband, slightly to the left of centre. The pleats should fall straight down. Pin the pleats to your petticoat with a safety pin to keep them in place all day.
Step 6
6
Step 06
Drape the Pallu — The Showpiece
Take the remaining fabric (the pallu — usually the most decorated end) and bring it from the right side, across the front, over the left shoulder. The pallu is the most visible and beautiful part of the saree — let it drape gracefully.
Step 7
7
Step 07
Adjust Length — Perfect Fall
Adjust the pallu length so it falls to about knee length at the back. Hold the pallu over your left shoulder and let the embroidered or printed end hang down at the back. You can also pleat the pallu for a neater look.
Step 8
8
Step 08
Pin the Shoulder — Stay Secure
Pin the pallu to your blouse at the shoulder with a safety pin or a decorative brooch. This keeps the pallu from slipping. You can also pin it at the back for added security. Make sure the drape looks natural and free-flowing.
💡 Tip: A decorative brooch at the shoulder doubles as a jewellery piece and keeps the pallu secure.
Step 9
9
Step 09
Final Adjustments — Perfect It
Walk a few steps and check that the lower hem is even and about 1 inch above the floor. Adjust the pleats if they have shifted. Check the front drape is smooth with no bunching. Secure any loose areas with additional pins hidden inside the folds.
Step 10
10
Step 10
The Complete Look — You're Ready!
Add your jewellery — bangles, earrings, a necklace, and a bindi. Slip on your heels or traditional footwear. Your handloom cotton saree is now beautifully draped. Walk with grace and confidence — a saree makes every woman unforgettable.
✨ A well-draped cotton saree needs no jewellery to shine — its texture, colour, and handwoven patterns speak for themselves.
