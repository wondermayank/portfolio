A Complete Guide

How to Wear
a Saree

Ten visual steps to drape a cotton saree with elegance — the Nivi style, beloved across India.

1
👘
Get Ready
Wear a well-fitted blouse and a petticoat tied firmly at the waist. The petticoat colour should match your saree. Stand straight and ensure the petticoat hem is just above ankle length.
2
📌
Tuck & Start
Hold the plain end of the saree and tuck it into the right side of your petticoat waistband. Go around the waist once from left to right, making sure the lower edge is just above the floor.
3
🔄
First Round Wrap
Continue wrapping the saree around your body, keeping the lower edge even. Make sure the fabric is smooth and not twisted. This first layer forms the base of your drape.
4
🪡
Make the Pleats
Gather the fabric into 5–7 neat pleats of about 5 inches each. Keep the pleats uniform and facing left. Hold them together firmly before tucking.
5
✦
Tuck the Pleats
Tuck the gathered pleats at the centre of the waistband, slightly tilting them to the left. The pleats should fall straight and open at the hem for graceful movement as you walk.
6
🌸
Bring the Pallu
After tucking the pleats, take the remaining fabric (the pallu) and bring it from behind around the right side, across the front, and up over the left shoulder.
7
💫
Drape the Pallu
Let the pallu fall naturally down your back. You may leave it loose for a casual look, or pleat it and pin it to the shoulder of your blouse with a decorative safety pin or brooch.
8
🔧
Adjust & Smooth
Walk a few steps and check the lower hem — it should be even all around. Smooth out any bunching at the waist. Re-tuck if needed to keep the drape clean and structured.
9
📿
Secure with Pins
Use safety pins at the waistband to secure the pleats and prevent unravelling. Pin the pallu at the left shoulder. For a blouse-to-saree pin, use a small hidden pin inside at the neckline.
10
👑
Final Touch
Add your jewellery — bangles, earrings, a necklace, and a bindi. Spritz on your fragrance. Stand tall, take a breath, and step out in the timeless grace of a perfectly draped cotton saree.
