The Craft

Where Threads
Carry Centuries

Each saree in the Zari Luxe collection is the result of weeks of painstaking work by master weavers. Real and imitation zari threads are woven into silk, georgette, and tissue to create patterns drawn from Mughal gardens, temple motifs, and peacock courts.

From the vibrant wine georgette to the delicate blush kota tissue, every drape tells a story that transcends seasons.
