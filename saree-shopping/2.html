A Complete Visual Guide

How to Wear
a Saree

Ten visual steps to drape a Georgette party saree with elegance — the classic Nivi style, beloved across India.

1 Step One Get Ready
Step 1
1
Step 01
Get Ready — Dress First
Wear a well-fitted blouse and a petticoat tied firmly at the waist. Choose a petticoat colour that closely matches your Georgette saree. Stand straight and ensure the petticoat hem falls just above ankle length for the best drape.
💡 A contrasting blouse can elevate the party look — try gold or silver against deep-coloured Georgette.
2
Step 02
First Tuck — Start Right
Tuck the plain end of the saree into the petticoat at the centre-front, and wrap once around your body anticlockwise. Georgette is slippery — tuck firmly and deeper than you would with cotton.
3
Step 03
Wrap Around — One Full Round
Continue wrapping the saree fully around your body from right to left. Keep the lower border even. Tuck the wrapped portion firmly into the petticoat waistband to secure the base. Georgette flows beautifully once anchored.
4
Step 04
Make Pleats — The Art
Take the remaining fabric and make 5–7 neat pleats of about 5 inches each. For Georgette, make pleats slightly wider for a fuller, more dramatic party look. Keep them even and facing left.
💡 Use a pleat-maker tool or a ruler to keep all folds perfectly even for a polished party appearance.
5
Step 05
Tuck Pleats — Secure Neatly
Hold all pleats together and tuck neatly into the waistband, slightly left of centre. Pin securely to your petticoat with a safety pin — essential for Georgette which is lighter and more prone to movement.
6
Step 06
Drape the Pallu — The Showpiece
Bring the pallu (the richly embellished end) from the right side, across the front, over the left shoulder. The Georgette pallu will drape with a natural, flowing elegance — this is the most glamorous moment of the drape.
7
Step 07
Adjust Length — Perfect Fall
Adjust the pallu length so it falls to knee level at the back. You can let it flow freely for a dramatic party look, or pleat it for a neater, more structured appearance. Both styles look stunning in Georgette.
8
Step 08
Pin the Shoulder — Stay Secure
Pin the pallu to your blouse at the shoulder — for Georgette, use at least two pins as the fabric is lighter. A decorative gold brooch at the shoulder adds a luxurious finishing touch perfect for parties.
💡 A statement brooch on the shoulder doubles as jewellery and keeps the Georgette pallu perfectly in place all night.
9
Step 09
Final Adjustments — Perfect It
Walk a few steps and verify the hem is even about 1 inch above the floor. Adjust pleats if shifted. Check the front drape is smooth. Secure any loose Georgette folds with additional hidden pins — Georgette is worth the extra care.
10
Step 10
The Complete Look — You're Ready!
Add your jewellery — statement earrings, bangles, a bold necklace, and a bindi. Slip on your heels. Your Georgette party wear saree is draped to perfection. Walk in — the party begins when you arrive.
✨ Georgette under warm party lights creates a luminous glow that no other fabric can replicate. You are unforgettable.
