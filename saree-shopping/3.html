The Art of Draping

How to Wear Your Banarasi Saree

1
Tuck the Petticoat

Begin by tucking the plain end of the saree into the petticoat waist, starting from your right side. Make one full wrap around.

2
Form the Pleats

Gather 5–7 neat pleats, each about 5 inches wide, folding them to the left. Pin them securely at the waist for an even fall.

3
Drape the Pallu

Bring the remaining fabric across your front, over your left shoulder. Let the zari pallu cascade elegantly down your back.

4
Pin the Pallu

Secure the pallu to your blouse at the shoulder with a pin so the brocade motifs remain beautifully visible throughout the ceremony.

5
Final Flourish

Adjust the border so it falls evenly. Pair with temple jewellery, a silk blouse in contrasting zari, and your bridal joy.
