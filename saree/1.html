How to Wear a Saree
✦
Step 01
Start & Tuck
Hold saree end at navel, tuck into petticoat waistband from left to right.
Step 02
Wrap Around
Bring saree all the way around the waist, lower edge just touching the floor.
Step 03
Make Pleats
Fold 5–7 pleats (~5 cm each) facing left, gathered at center front.
Step 04
Tuck Pleats
Tuck pleats into waistband slightly left of center, keep them straight.
Step 05
Drape the Pallu
Bring remaining fabric across front, drape pallu over left shoulder and pin.
