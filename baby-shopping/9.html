Our Story

Rooted in craft,
made for new life.

Haathmade Baby began in a small workshop in Jaipur, where a grandmother's tradition of hand-stitching baby clothes met a mother's desire to give her child only the purest things.

We work with 40+ artisan families across Rajasthan, Gujarat and Tamil Nadu — keeping traditional crafts alive while making products safe for the most sensitive skin.

— Meera Sharma, Founder
