प्रक्रिया · The Making

Every pair passes through three pairs of hands.

The Chamar tans the hide with kikar bark until it turns supple. The Rangaar dyes and colours it by eye, matching shades passed down without a swatch book. The Mochi stitches the panna, sole, and chinkiya together entirely by hand.

No pair leaves Amritsar or Jodhpur until all three sign off on the work — a promise we've kept since 1962.
