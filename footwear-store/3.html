Three stages, one last

Nothing here is automated. The stages below are the same three a shoe has always passed through in this workshop.

I

The Last

A beechwood form is shaped to the individual foot — the permanent architecture every future pair will be built on.

II

The Welt

Upper, insole, and welt are hand-stitched together — the join that lets a sole be replaced without retiring the shoe.

III

The Finish

Weeks of hand-polishing raise the patina — the depth of colour a machine buff can't replicate.
