कारीगरी · The Fusion Process

One shoe, two lineages.

We pair Amritsar's tilla-embroidery ateliers with sneaker-grade sole engineers in the same production run — so the upper carries four centuries of thread-work, and the sole carries this decade's cushioning tech.

No pair leaves the workshop until both artisans sign off — the embroiderer on the panna, the cobbler on the flex. That's the fusion promise.
