The Build

Four stages, one pair of feet.

I.

Measure

21 points across both feet, taken standing and seated, plus a pressure scan for gait and weight distribution.

II.

Last

A beechwood last is turned to those exact measurements — the mold every future pair for you will share.

III.

Craft

Leather is cut, skived, and hand-stitched over the last using a traditional Goodyear welt construction.

IV.

Finish

Edges are burnished, the sole is trimmed and stained, and the upper is hand-polished to a museum shine.
